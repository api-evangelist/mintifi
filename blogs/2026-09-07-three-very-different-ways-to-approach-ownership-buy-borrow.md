The way a business acquires an asset can matter as much as the asset itself. Buying commits capital to ownership; borrowing adds a financing obligation; leasing can give businesses access to the assets they need while preserving greater flexibility over their capital and future choices. The right question, therefore, is not simply how to acquire an asset, but whether owning it is the best way to create value from it.
